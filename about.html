01 THE IDEA

One space. Many ways to create.

Hazel is an independent creative studio built around curiosity, imagination and the belief that creativity should never be confined to a single discipline.

From digital experiences and visual design to storytelling and motion, Hazel explores different ways of bringing ideas to life. Each project is an opportunity to experiment, solve problems and create something meaningful.

02 OUR CREATIVE WORLD

Hazel moves between different creative fields, finding connections between ideas that might otherwise exist in separate worlds.

04 THE PERSON BEHIND IT

A little bit of everything. And a lot of imagination.

Hazel is currently a one-person creative studio, founded and run by someone who has never been particularly good at choosing just one thing to do.

I enjoy exploring different creative disciplines, learning new skills and turning random ideas into actual projects. Hazel is where all those interests meet, grow and take shape.

It's still evolving, and that's part of the point. There's always something new to explore, something new to create and another idea worth giving a chance.
